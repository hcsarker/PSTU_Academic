Length of packet (L) = 1 thousand bytes

Link distance (d) = 2500km

Speed of propagation (s) = 2.5* 10^8 m/s

Transmission rate (R) = 2 Mbps = 2,000,000 bits/sec

1 byte = 8 bits

Transmission delay = L/R

                                = 8 * 1,000 / 2,000,000 

                                = 4 milliseconds


Propagation delay = d/s

                              = 2,500 / 2.5×105

                              = 10 milliseconds


Hence, 

Finding total time by adding transmission and propagation delay.

Total time required = 4 + 10  = 14 milliseconds.
